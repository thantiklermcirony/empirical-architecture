# A science of adequate descriptions

Science makes the world intelligible by distinguishing some differences and forgetting others. A state, a variable, a law and a model all contain decisions about which differences matter.

The Empirical Architecture makes those decisions an explicit subject of mathematics, experiment and engineering.

Its ambition is a revolution in scientific representation and discovery: a common framework through which scientists can establish what their models must remember, discover how interventions transform a system, and determine which conclusions and actions their evidence supports.

## The present has a history

A living cell is not exhausted by its present concentration. An ecosystem is not exhausted by its abundance histogram. An intervention is not exhausted by its ingredient list. An intelligent system is not exhausted by the sentence it produces.

In each case, history and structure can determine consequences that the displayed summary cannot distinguish. The remedy is an experimentally justified description that retains the distinctions required by the question.

A predictive state collects histories that every declared future test treats equivalently. This offers a precise account of what may be forgotten. It also explains why new instruments can change the empirical state space: a distinction becomes available that the older observer could not read.

## Change has a language

Interventions can be composed. Their order may matter; their domains may depend on state; waiting may transform their effects. A cumulative quantity is adequate only when these operations support that compression.

The programme investigates the language of change: cumulative actions, temporal grammar, nonlinear composition and geometric structure. UHL belongs here through its developed composition laws, classification results and continuing mathematical questions.

The latest Predictive Closure architecture supplies an explicit fork between cumulative and order-sensitive descriptions. It also supplies routes toward reconstructing predictive state from the responses that an experiment can observe. A small architecture can therefore support different scientific structures without forcing every system into the same equation.

## Life changes the conditions of its own continuation

Living systems regulate resources, preserve some distinctions, erase others, adapt to perturbation and alter their environment. Their actions help construct the constraints encountered by later states.

The programme asks how this recursive process produces capability, adaptation, vulnerability and recovery. Hormesis, redox homeostasis, ecological dynamics and genomic context become linked questions about mechanism, history and attainable futures. Their shared architecture creates opportunities for joint predictions; their mechanisms still have to be identified.

## Intelligence should be organized around its encounter with the world

An AI can propose an explanation without having established it. It can repeat a correct fact while losing the context that makes it relevant. It can predict an outcome without possessing the information or authority needed to act.

The programme separates generation, evidence, state and action so that each function can be investigated and improved. The long-term objective is a scientific system that notices when its representation fails, proposes alternatives, designs a useful test and maintains its conclusions as knowledge changes.

This creates an architectural and economic question: how much useful intelligence must reside in the proposer, and how much can be supplied by explicit evidence, tools, memory and interaction? The answer requires implementation and comparison. A substantial shift would change how AI is built and where its value resides.

## Consciousness is part of the research programme

The observer is central to empirical science, and experience is central to the human observer. IDA provides a route toward investigating cognitive state through response to intervention, context, history and recovery.

Reports, metacognition, flexible access, physiological dynamics and subjective experience require carefully stated relationships. The programme seeks experiments that distinguish proposed mechanisms. Its architecture does not itself identify a neural signal with consciousness.

## Breadth must produce obligations

A framework spanning science should make work more exact. It should identify a missing measurement, derive an unexpected restriction, connect predictions across experiments, reveal a false equivalence or enable a useful instrument.

Each domain contributes tests of the larger architecture. Each failed application should revise a specific claim. A mathematical proof, a simulation, a retrospective finding and a prospective experiment have different roles, and their relationships should remain visible.

The project is open to independent contributions that improve any part of this structure. Agreement with the entire philosophical programme is not a prerequisite for reproducing a calculation, challenging a theorem or building a better instrument.

## The commitment

We intend to build the mathematics, software, instruments and community needed to make scientific description itself more rigorous and more capable.

The scale of the ambition is science-wide. Its progress will be expressed in results people can inspect, reproduce, criticize and use.
