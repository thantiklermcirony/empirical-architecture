# Build the architecture through results

## Available in this source tree

- A public statement of the programme and its philosophical scope.
- A 41-record corpus index and the existing 41 principal test families.
- Four offline mathematical/synthetic reference examples and checks.
- A research map joining foundations, scientific applications, AI and IDA.
- A compact UHL publication and replacement strategy.

## First release: a reproducible entrance

Make the repository understandable to a new reader. Ensure the examples run on a clean Python installation. Identify the current foundation and connect historical sources. Accept contributions that reproduce, explain or challenge a specific result.

Completion means a person outside the project can understand the central claim, run the examples and identify a useful contribution without private assistance.

## Second release: the AI architecture under comparison

Implement a real proposer adapter and freeze a versioned evaluation. Compare equivalent evidence access and multiple model tiers. Publish coverage, correctness, costs and failures. Account for all calls and any powerful model used for checking.

Completion means independent reproduction of a practically useful result, or a clear report that a specified architecture/workload claim failed. A synthetic checker is not this milestone.

## Third release: scientific state revision

Connect prediction failure, candidate state refinement, experiment selection and revised inference. Use a controlled finite laboratory with distinct sources of model failure, then evaluate on a separate empirical task. Reuse IDA's existing atlas and experiment concepts where appropriate.

Completion means state revision earns a measured predictive or decision advantage beyond a strong fixed-representation comparison.

## Scientific campaigns

The biological campaign connects mechanism, adaptation and recoverability. The ecological campaign identifies what time-ordered data add beyond stationary summaries. Genome work tests turnover and context. Cognition/consciousness work specifies rival mechanism-to-outcome predictions. Mathematical and physical campaigns resolve named proof obligations.

These campaigns share interfaces and methods; their result schedules depend on collaborator access, data and the mathematics. There are no invented completion dates or promised breakthroughs.

## Community milestones

Track independent reproductions, recurring users, external contributions, resolved counterexamples, reusable integrations and empirical collaborations. Releases should foreground an inspectable result and a short explanation people can share. Popularity is a distribution measure; evidence determines scientific claims.
