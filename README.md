# Empirical Architecture

**Daniel John Murray · Foundations of the Empirical Architecture Observatory**

A research programme about systems and observers acting within finite capacities: what a measurement preserves, what it conceals, and when the missing distinction changes a prediction or an available action.

**[Explore the public research exhibits](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/)** · [Papers](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/) · [Predictions and tests](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/predictions/) · [Laboratory source](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory)

## The scientific account

A measured response, the resources supporting it, and the ability to restore those resources are different quantities. Two systems can look alike now and respond differently to the next challenge. An observer must discover which distinctions matter, retain an adequate state description, and act while useful options remain.

The programme connects several kinds of work. Each connection needs an explicit mathematical reduction, a shared mechanism or an empirical test.

This supplies a common architecture for building and comparing models. A complete, empirically established theory of nature remains an ambition.

## Results and evidence

- **Published biological models:** [Hormesis, Dose-Response](https://doi.org/10.1177/15593258261469171) and [glutathione homeostasis, Redox Biochemistry and Chemistry](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rbc.2026.100084). Their conclusions depend on their stated equations and assumptions. Kinetic calibration and prospective cancer predictions have different evidential status.
- **Conditional mathematical results:** [proofs and certificates](research/theory-atlas-v0.1/PROOFS.md) cover composition, state, resources, action and their connections. [Source findings](research/theory-atlas-v0.1/SOURCE_FINDINGS.md) record corrections and limits.
- **Completed comparisons:** [Recovery Lab](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory/tree/main/research/recovery-lab), [Virtual Cell flight 01](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory/tree/main/research/virtual-cell) and [flight 02](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory/tree/main/research/virtual-cell-flight02) preserve the data, comparisons and failed success gates.
- **Engineering:** [the Observatory repository](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory) connects runnable tools, reproductions and upstream contributions. Passing software checks does not establish the wider scientific theory.

The [catalogue](corpus/README.md) contains 41 manuscript records, including historical versions. These are not 41 independently validated discoveries. Bounds alone do not select one geometry; order effects alone do not prove every scalar state model fails; a resource fraction does not measure the absolute reserve. The corrected assumptions belong beside the results.

## Reproduce or improve one result

Choose a result, read its assumptions, run the linked reproduction, and compare it with an appropriate alternative. Useful contributions include counterexamples, independent calibrations, stronger baselines and tests that distinguish mechanisms.
