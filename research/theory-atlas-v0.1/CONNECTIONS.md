## 1. A diagnostic can consume the opportunity it is intended to identify

**Structures.** A nonnegative stoichiometric state trajectory, a finite-horizon resource inequality, a two-hypothesis statistical experiment, and disjoint acceptable-action sets. This connects current-05/11 with the finite regeneration mechanism, and can later feed TAO scheduling and IDA restoration checks.

**Map and preserved relations.** Map the physical state to B=GSH/2+NADPH. This linear functional preserves the declared reaction accounting while discarding rates and compartment details. Integrating its derivative gives a necessary resource-duration condition. Map a diagnostic record law to optimal action-selection error via total variation. Finally intersect the admissible time set with the diagnostic duration/error set. Resource amount is never identified with information; duration is their shared interface.

**Useful computation.** The closed-source induction example excludes every T>2. For equal-prior iid Bernoulli3/4 versus1/4 records, seven samples are the first fixed-count test to reach error<=1/10. At one sample per1/2 time unit it takes7/2, beyond the resource bound. At most four samples fit the upper horizon and their best error is5/32. This proves an exclusion for the declared diagnostic family. Increasing sampling speed to one per1/4 makes it merely not excluded; it does not prove resource feasibility, acceptable measurement disturbance, or successful rescue. Code and supplied proofs are in `certify_resources.py`, `certify_action_evidence.py` and `PROOFS.md`.

**Nearest primary work.** Combining control and diagnosis is established in [Sampath, Lafortune and Teneketzis, 1998](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~teneket/pubs/ActiveDiagnosisOfDES-1998.pdf). Diagnosis before unsafe evolution is explicitly formalized by [Paoli and Lafortune, 2005](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0005109805001111). A much richer biochemical model with synthesis, transport and breakdown appears in [Reed et al., 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2391141/). The atlas neither invents safe diagnosis nor replaces such biochemistry; it supplies an exact, inspectable bridge between separate contracts.

**Unresolved premise.** Can the target biological unit provide a sufficiently fast record whose conditional likelihood, disturbance, duration and future action availability are known while the resource bound remains valid? The biology lead's present reference is synthetic. Its inspected public retina data are cross-sectional/destructive and do not supply this joint test. Quantum product yield cannot be substituted for a chemical flux without rate, stoichiometry, compartment and time calibration.

**Decisive test.** Independently calibrate absolute pools, source/regeneration ceilings, input demand and diagnostic record laws. Freeze the action library, time accounting and target error. Predict an excluded region of input duration versus assay speed before running held-out schedules. Include an external-source or faster-diagnostic control that moves the predicted boundary. A nominally excluded successful trajectory challenges at least one resource/measurement premise; it does not disprove conservation. Failure of a particular rescue policy does not prove that all policies fail.

## 2. A composition invariant can survive when order matters

**Structures.** Positive homogeneous two-channel states modulo common scale; real projective transformations; the semigroup of ordered operations; and cross ratios of four states. Current-07 supplies the observation lift, current-06 supplies mechanism-selected operation families, and current-10 separates action composition from count compression. TAO's different primitive families offer an engineering application.

**Map and preserved relations.** Project z to q=z1/z2 and then, if useful, to the bounded contrast x=(q-1)/(q+1). A common invertible linear lift gives a fractional-linear map wherever its chart is valid. Cross ratio survives this projection and a common chart change. Full ordered maps remain associative under composition. Commutation is an additional property; endpoint-fixing UHL is a narrower subgroup.

**Useful computation.** A(q)=2q and B(q)=q+1 yield2q+1 versus2q+2 in opposite orders. Both preserve cross ratio4/3 on q=(1,2,3,4), also after conversion to the bounded chart. Thus “order effect” and “projective” are compatible, and an order effect need not imply hidden state. B is a forward map into the positive domain, not an onto endpoint-fixing automorphism. The example prevents incorrectly rejecting an entire projective model when only its abelian UHL specialization fails.

**Nearest primary work.** Cross ratios as projective invariants are classical; [Anderson, 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.02065) explicitly derives them as preserved quantities and identifies their established status. Projective superposition for Riccati dynamics has a separate control-theory history, illustrated by [D'Alessandro, 1998](https://scholar.lib.vt.edu/ejournals/JMSEC/v8n1/92925.pdf). General additive interval composition has a different hypothesis set in [Aczel, 1948](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/bsmf.1396.pdf). The proposed advance is arranging these known structures into falsifiable, source-bound branches, not renaming them as a new universal mechanism.

**Unresolved premise.** The physical readout must support a deterministic point map at the declared resolution and use the same calibration across operations and histories. Otherwise a four-point invariant can be distorted by noise, pooling or missing state. Positive diagonal quantum filtering supplies a conditional example only when success mass is retained; coherent propagation still needs phase.

**Decisive test.** Fit/calibrate each operation independently, freeze parameters, and predict held-out four-point transformations plus AB/BA outcomes. Separately test point-map adequacy, cross-ratio residuals and commutation with simultaneous uncertainty. Passing a finite battery supports only that battery. If each map and ordered prediction pass but commutation fails, keep the nonabelian projective branch; if matched present states have different common futures, enlarge the state before changing the geometry. The exact square-map counterexample guards against inferring projectivity from bounded monotonicity.

## 3. Waiting can expose modes that every immediate quadratic challenge misses

**Structures.** A quadratic state potential under a fixed loading direction, a positive mixture of exponential recovery modes, scalar semigroup composition, and an operational leaky return score. This connects current-03 to current-10 time transport and IDA's recovery metrics without making every notion of residue the same object.

**Map and preserved relations.** Immediate challenge increments G(s+cd)-G(s) with quadratic G depend on s through one projection c^T A s. That projection can merge different states while preserving every immediate challenge amplitude. During waiting, distinct modal rates change their contributions. If the calibrated readout is m(t)=sum w_i exp(-lambda_i t), fixed positive weights give m(2t)-m(t)^2=Var_w(exp(-lambda_i t)). A one-exponential amplitude semigroup has zero defect. The map preserves the declared readout and its time law; it does not preserve every hidden coordinate.

**Useful computation.** Equal weights and unit attenuations1/2,1/4 give m(1)=3/8,m(2)=5/32 and defect1/64. A gap ladder therefore targets an identifiable failure that increasing only immediate challenge amplitudes may never reveal. IDA can retain this feature for held-out prediction. It cannot silently replace it with energy: `certify_connections.py` gives trajectories whose linear leaky residue decreases while quadratic residue increases.

**Nearest primary work.** Dose-equivalent incomplete repair is already developed by [Thames, 1985](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/09553008514550461), with a generalized fractionated low-dose-rate treatment in [Nilsson, Thames and Joiner, 1990](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1967284/). These are relevant antecedents, not proofs of the proposed biology or of every multimode assumption. The variance identity is elementary. The novel opportunity, if any, is a useful preregistered assay or model-selection procedure exploiting the distinction between immediate and temporal closure.

**Unresolved premise.** Fixed nonnegative weights and a stationary linear modal readout must be earned. Nonlinear frailty pooling, changing population composition, signed modes, dose-dependent recovery or drifting reference can make the same measured defect mean something else. The result rejects a scalar linear amplitude semigroup, not all scalar nonlinear dynamics.

**Decisive test.** Use independent preparation/readout calibration; measure t and2t plus untouched gaps; retain the same unit/population definition; compare a single exponential, positive mixture and justified nonlinear/heterogeneity alternatives with the same data split. Propagate joint uncertainty into m(2t)-m(t)^2. Demand held-out future improvement over static level and simple time-since-exposure before admitting the feature as useful. A defect near zero only excludes modes resolvable by this readout and window.

## Other prior-work checks and search limits

The predictive-state branch overlaps [Littman, Sutton and Singh, 2001](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper/1983-predictive-representations-of-state.pdf), which uses action-conditional future predictions as state. Reversal-graph potentials and residual cycles belong to the framework studied by [Jiang, Lim, Yao and Ye, 2011](https://www.web.stanford.edu/~yyye/hodgeRank2011.pdf). Evidence-carrying execution has substantial ancestry in proof-carrying code; [Necula and Schneck, 2002](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~necula/ISSS02/) explicitly treats the trusted rule base. These overlaps support reuse, while preventing claims of mathematical priority based on changed terminology.
