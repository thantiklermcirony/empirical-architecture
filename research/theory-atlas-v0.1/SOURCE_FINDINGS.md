## Implementation correction found during review

The initial evidence helper checked only premise IDs supplied by its witness. A witness with an empty list could bypass false required premises. The final helper obtains the required set from its trusted rule definition, rejects omitted/erased dependencies and absent/false required premises, and retains the positive case. The final admission adapter also separates fixed-fixture arithmetic from application to a changed inquiry. These are implementation corrections, distinct from F1-F8.
