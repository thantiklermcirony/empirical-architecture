# UHL in the larger architecture

UHL is the historical and continuing composition/geometry line within The Empirical Architecture. The current programme places it after state and action admission, and develops its mathematical content through explicit branches and physical applications.

The original collection should be reorganized around a compact current foundation, with historical papers preserved and linked to their successors. Archiving a repository does not withdraw every result it contains. Replacing a proof or changing a claim requires a specific revision note.

## Mathematical extensions

The classification's two-dimensional SO(2) case is an explicit bounded starting problem. Other independent targets include radial-gauge relaxation, properties of the three-dimensional chiral family and carefully specified anisotropic constructions. Each needs a clear theorem or counterexample, with computational exploration supporting the argument.

The empirical geometry programme must distinguish three different objects: ordinary order dependence of action maps, reassociation defects of a reduced composition law, and curvature in an independently justified smooth structure. Function composition remains associative. The relevant physical or observational representation must identify which of these is being measured.

## Physical extensions

The Dirac, Clifford, quantum-bound and Yang–Mills manuscripts require their additional dynamics and representations to be explicit. The newer Yang–Mills development targets physical ground-state Poincaré control with the appropriate kinetic form and scale-uniform estimates. A finite-system gap certificate can advance that programme without establishing the continuum result.

The next physical paper should close a named gap: a defined regulated-family bound, a comparison theorem, a representation result or a justified limit. A hyperbolic coordinate change alone does not supply those results.

## Historical transition

The earlier one-PDF repositories are being consolidated into this programme. Their [PDF versions and provenance](../history/README.md) remain available here, with full repository snapshots and Git-history bundles retained in the owner’s backup. Seventeen historical repositories were selected for removal after the replacement archive was verified. SSRN records remain the manuscript citation destinations. Specific claim corrections should remain attached to the original claim and successor.

The detailed proposed repository-by-repository migration is maintained in the publication package. Removing a repository does not by itself retract the corresponding manuscript or establish a successor proof.
