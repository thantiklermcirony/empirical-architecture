# The whole scientific programme

The project studies a common architecture of scientific description and action. Its philosophical reach is the relationship between an underlying world, an observer's access to it, the representations that preserve its consequences, and the actions that change its future.

## The current foundation

The latest **Predictive Closure, Version 4** develops an explicit architecture: predictive state, descended action, an exact cumulative/temporal fork, scalar descent where valid, temporal grammar where order matters, and global predictive realization. Its local smooth branch includes curvature and challenge-visible reconstruction. These are existing manuscript contents; they are not described here as future conceptual inventions. [SSRN record](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7427098).

**The Observer and the World** connects state-law descent with minimal predictive state, changes in experimental access and conditional projective observation structure. **Biological State Sufficiency** makes matched-present/common-future experiments a test of what a biological measurement omits. **Temporal Architecture** incorporates histories selected by constraints, viable continuations and actions that modify the environment. **From Predictive State to Viable Action** separates prediction, action-relevant information and safely obtainable distinctions. [Observer](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7426998), [biological state](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7425878), [temporal architecture](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7426938), [viable action](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7427100).

**Bounded Composition Classification** supplies the stated flat/Einstein classification under its specified inverse, smoothness, symmetry, defect-isometry and radial assumptions. Other composition papers establish the role of mechanism-selected generators and additional geometric structure. [Classification](https://ssrn.com/abstract=6914800), [Aczél-family pharmacology](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7426978), [geometric enrichment](https://ssrn.com/abstract=6800400).

This mathematical architecture provides restrictions and investigative methods. Applications add mechanisms, measurements and empirical questions.

## Shared research objects

Every scientific contribution should declare its system and access, state representation, admitted actions, outcomes, uncertainty, relevant resource/time constraints, assumptions, evidence and decisive test. A theorem records its premises and proof. An experiment records its actual preparation and observation procedures. Software records the scope of what it computes or checks.

A new result should connect to the larger programme through an explicit relationship: logical implication, conditional reduction, empirical test, reusable implementation or shared methodological problem. Similar vocabulary alone is not a dependency.

The planned scientific engine uses that structure to help generate explanations, select experiments, revise state and maintain conclusions. IDA brings the engine into intervention-response research. The AI component is also itself a test of the architecture.
